# Playing games that use anti-cheat

Before launching a protected game, right-click **KeyRewind** in the Windows notification area and choose **Exit**. If the icon is hidden, open **Show hidden icons** first. To confirm the process has closed, open **Task Manager → Details** and check that `KeyRewind.exe` is gone. Reopen `KeyRewind.exe` after playing to restore your desktop shortcuts.

Use **Gaming Mode** in the tray menu when you want to pause the shortcuts and keep KeyRewind open. A violet dot on the tray icon and a check mark next to **Gaming Mode** show that the keyboard, mouse, and focus hooks have been released. KeyRewind remembers this choice across restarts. Click **Gaming Mode** again to restore your shortcuts. Windows layout switching with **Win + Space** remains available while shortcuts are paused. [Windows keyboard shortcuts](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accessibility/windows/keyboard-shortcuts-in-windows)

Compatibility while KeyRewind is open depends on the game and its anti-cheat version.
